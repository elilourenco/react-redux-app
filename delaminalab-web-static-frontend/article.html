Notes on making

22 August 2026 · By de la Mina Lab

This is the static counterpart to the WordPress single-post template. It is a flexible page for articles, artist notes, or project stories.

Content is deliberately simple: a title, metadata, rich text, and optional visual sections. Once the design is settled, WordPress can provide this title and post content dynamically.

The static branch can have more article files when you need to test editorial variations without changing the production theme.
